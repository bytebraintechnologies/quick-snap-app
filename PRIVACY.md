# Privacy

Quick Snap processes screenshots, recordings, annotations, clipboard data, and exports locally on
the user's device. It does not upload captured content to Bytebrain.

The application does not include advertising or application-usage telemetry. Optional operating
system integrations and external tools, including FFmpeg and Microsoft Store, operate under their
respective terms.

Preferences, capture history, and diagnostic logs are stored locally. Diagnostic logs do not contain
capture or clipboard content, but users should still review files before attaching them to a public
support issue.

This summary applies to the current Quick Snap application. Material changes will be documented here
and in the relevant release notes.
